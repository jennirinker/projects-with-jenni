Defense
===============


I recommend you re-read Lars Landberg's book before your defense to refamiliarize yourself with what a censor is looking for.

Finding a censor
------------------

* I'm responsible for finding a censor suited for your project.  
* Remind me two months before you hand in to find a censor for you.  
* If I still haven't confirmed a censor one month before you hand in, remind me again!  


Scheduling the defense
-----------------------

* Once the censor is confirmed, I will make an email thread with you, all supervisors, and the censor to coordinate schedules.  
* The defense should happen within 10 working days of the hand-in.  
* I will book the meeting room as well.  


Presentation
-------------

* A defence presentation is typically 30 minutes long.  
* The structure should be similar to your report: what is the problem you are trying to solve, how did you try to solve it, and how well did your method work.  

Here is an excellent example of how one can structure a defence presentation that clearly defines and highlights the “story” behind the work (requires DTU login): `example MSc defence <https://panopto.dtu.dk/Panopto/Pages/Viewer.aspx?id=45320e4b-d3dd-4d07-ac51-b29800e3ac4f>`_.
