Think like a researcher
========================

Doing a thesis (or other research project) requires a different mindset than what you're used to in courses.
Here, you're responsible for...  

* Surveying what's already been done and identifying important gaps  
* Proposing methods to address important gaps in the state of the art
* Implementing and critically evaluating said methods, being especially aware of key limiting assumptions
* Soliciting feedback from your supervisors and using it to make decisions on where to go


Research questions
--------------------

* Your work, when it's complete, should answer one or more research questions.  
* Research questions almost always evolve as a project progresses.  
* Research questions are closely tied to your :ref:`literature_survey`.  
* When drafting/revising your research questions, I recommend considering the following:  

    * **State of the art**. What have previous researchers found in the area of this research question?  
    * **Gaps**. What is needed in the broader context and has NOT been done? I.e., what is your target?  
    * **Hypothesis**. What do you guess will be the answer to the research question?  
    * **How to prove your hypothesis**. What experiments/analysis will you do that will prove/disprove your hypothesis? Already think about what plots you might make!
